# 💰 Gender Pricing Transparency Engine

### **From Calculator to Crowdsourced Data Audit**

A comprehensive, open-source platform that transforms gender pricing disparities from abstract numbers into tangible, verifiable economic evidence. This is not just a calculator—it's a global database documenting structural economic inequality.

---

## 🎯 What This Project Does

**The Problem:** Women pay 7-40% more for identical products due to gendered marketing. This "Pink Tax" is structural, undisclosed, and rarely quantified.

**Our Solution:** A crowdsourced transparency engine where users submit real-world evidence—photos of price tags, store locations, products—to build a global dataset of economic disparities.

**Impact:** By aggregating this data regionally, we can:
- Provide policymakers with empirical evidence for regulation
- Show journalists quantifiable stories about gender economics
- Help consumers understand the true cost of gender-based pricing
- Bridge technical innovation with public policy research

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. **Crowdsourced Data Audit Database**
- Users submit real-world pricing evidence with photos
- Each submission is tagged by region, product category, and store
- Database displays all submissions with calculated markup percentages
- Searchable and filterable by region, category, and markup threshold

### 2. **Regional Pricing Analysis**
- Compare gender-pricing disparities across UAE, MENA, Asia, Europe, and beyond
- Identify which regions have higher/lower markups
- Understand local economic factors affecting pricing
- Bridges global trends with local context (key for international scholarship apps!)

### 3. **Policy Integration**
- Executive banner linking to your research/policy breakdown
- Methodology section grounded in NYC Department of Consumer Affairs research
- Frames the "why" (policy & economics) alongside the "how" (code)

### 4. **Quick Calculator**
- Still available! Calculate lifetime cost of Pink Tax
- Shows annual overspend and 50-year impact

### 5. **Technical Features**
- 100% client-side storage (localStorage) — no backend needed
- Fully responsive design
- Dark, professional UI optimized for serious economic research
- Pre-loaded with sample data from UAE, UK, India

---

## 📊 Data Structure

Each submission includes:
- **Product Info:** Name, category, male/female prices
- **Location Data:** Store, city, region, currency
- **Evidence:** Photos of price tags side-by-side
- **Context:** Description, store name, submitter info
- **Calculated:** Automatic markup % based on price difference
