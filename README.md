⁠# 📉 Gender Pricing Transparency Engine (Pink Tax Calculator)

An independent open-source digital tool designed to quantify economic disparities and gender-based price markups on consumer goods. 

## 🎯 Purpose & Impact
Consumer research frequently highlights systemic price discrepancies where products marketed toward women carry inflated price tags compared to standard or male-marketed equivalents. This digital utility transforms static economic research into an interactive, open-access tool, allowing users to instantly audit price markups, absolute cost differences, and projected annual overspends.

## 💻 Features
* **Category Filtering:** Sort data across Personal Care, Apparel, and Children's Goods.
* **Instant Disparity Audit:** Calculates precise markup percentages and absolute currency deltas.
* **Annual Impact Projection:** Projects long-term consumer financial drain over a 12-month purchasing cycle.

## 🚀 Live Access
You can run and test the interactive engine live via [GitHub Pages](https://moonayasab-droid.github.io/gender-pricing-transparency-engine/pink-tax.html).
