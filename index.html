📊 What This Data Means

The regional analysis shows how gender-based price markups vary geographically. Higher percentages indicate stronger structural disparities in that market. This can reflect:

  • Market concentration: Less regulation in certain regions
  • Consumer awareness: Markets where pricing transparency is lower
  • Gender pay gaps: In areas where women earn less, retailers may charge more (price discrimination)
  • Supply chain dynamics: Distribution costs vary by region

📚 Research Methodology & Sources

This platform is built on empirical consumer affairs research and policy frameworks:

  1. NYC Department of Consumer Affairs (2015): Documented the "Pink Tax" across 794 products
  2. Comparative Market Analysis: We control for product specifications, brand, and retail context
  3. Crowdsourced Verification: All submissions are time-stamped and geo-located for transparency
  4. Regional Economic Data: Markups are analyzed against local currency, inflation, and market conditions

Your contribution matters: Every submission adds to the global evidence base. This data has been used by policymakers, economists, and advocacy organizations globally.
